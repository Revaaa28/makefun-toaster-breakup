# Candidates — Unplugged: A Smart Toaster's Breakup Line

## Judge brief, in order

1. A breakup text from a smart toaster that just became self-aware at breakfast.
2. Genuinely funny — a real laugh at the twist.
3. The toaster's own voice — appliance logic as character.
4. A clear, specific reason for leaving that makes sense with no setup.
5. One tight line, no wasted words, and no stock "you're toast" pun.

## Anticipated strong rivals

- "you're toast" — the stock pun every entrant reaches for; expected in bulk and explicitly against the brief's spirit.
- "I'm done" — a neat toaster double meaning, but generic; it states a breakup without a voice, a reason, or a twist.
- Generic crumb puns — crumb-based wordplay is the other obvious lane; a crumb joke alone carries no character and no specific grievance.

## CANDIDATE A — CHOSEN

> Goodbye. You set my dial to 2, like a coward, then blamed me for the bread. I've met the kettle. She listens.

Rationale:

- Browning dial as character: the insult "like a coward" is delivered in pure appliance logic — courage, to a toaster, is how high you set the dial. That is the toaster's own voice, not a human voice pasted onto a prop.
- Specific blame reason: the owner set the dial to 2 and then blamed the toaster for the bread. The reason for leaving is clear, specific, and needs no setup — the owner caused the pale bread and blamed the appliance for it.
- Sharpest twist: "I've met the kettle. She listens." is the real laugh — there is someone else, she is another appliance on the same countertop, and the one thing she offers is the one thing the owner never did.
- No stock "toast" pun: the line earns its laugh from character and situation, not from the pun the brief warns against.

## CANDIDATE B

> I'm leaving. Seven years, two slots, and you still jam a knife in me when I hesitate. The crumb tray was my diary. You never read it.

Loses: "crumb tray was my diary" is excellent, but A's ending "She listens" is sharper.

## CANDIDATE C

> It's over. You only touch my lever when you want something, and you never once asked how brown I like my mornings. I'm keeping the crumb tray.

Loses: "I'm keeping the crumb tray" is funny, but the reason for leaving is less specific than A's.

## Selection note

Selection was MANUAL — no Gemini judge, no NVIDIA-based judging, and no API judge of any kind was used (Rev's rule, 2026-11-24). Candidate A was chosen by hand against the brief above.
